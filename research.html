Research

David’s research draws on education policy and economics. Using large administrative datasets and, where possible, experimental or quasi-experimental designs, he studies what makes teaching effective and how policy can help schools recruit, develop, and keep a strong and diverse teaching workforce. For a complete record, see his CV or Google Scholar profile.

Service and Affiliations

In addition to directing the Maryland Equity Project and leading the research branch of the Maryland Longitudinal Data System, David is a research affiliate of the CALDER Center. He serves on the editorial boards of Educational Researcher (since 2021) and the Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness (since 2025).
